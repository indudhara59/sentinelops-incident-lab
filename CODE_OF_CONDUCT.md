# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and learning-focused community.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating impact, give specific technical feedback, respect privacy, and make space for different experience levels. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person’s private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines safety or collaboration. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of affected participants.
