# Product principles

1. **Safe by construction** — all systems, telemetry, commands, and outcomes are simulated.
2. **Evidence over guessing** — learning flows should reward defensible hypotheses.
3. **Operational realism** — scenarios model service relationships and signals without touching real systems.
4. **Honest scope** — unfinished capabilities are labeled, never presented as working features.
5. **Accessible learning** — keyboard support, strong contrast, reduced motion, and plain language are defaults.
