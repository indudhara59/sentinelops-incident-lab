# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (complete)

Monorepo tooling, public homepage, design system, accessible themes, shared contracts, and hardened FastAPI health/status service.

## Phase 2 — Catalog and briefing (complete)

Typed scenario definitions, searchable and filterable catalog, five fictional incident briefings, solution-data boundary, commander checklist, and safe local handoff to a placeholder route.

## Phase 3 — Live operations workspace (not started)

Potential scope: deterministic simulated topology and telemetry panels, evidence collection, and safe corrective-action UI. Architecture and safety review are required before implementation.

## Later phases (not started)

Potential persistence, authentication, scoring, post-incident reports, custom scenario creation, and deployment. These are not available and no dates are promised.
